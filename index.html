Co-Design and AI-Supported Storytelling for Maternal and Child Health in Sierra Leone

Bringing African oral storytelling traditions together with digital health tools, so that mothers, health workers, elders and storytellers can author the health stories their communities share.

Sampson Kofi Adotey, PhD Candidate University of Cape Town Fieldwork: Makeni, Sierra Leone Supervised by Assoc. Prof. Melissa Densmore and Dr. Irina Catrinel Crăciun
The problem

Preventable deaths, every few minutes

Every 2 minutes

a mother dies from preventable childbirth complications.

Every minute

a child under five dies from treatable diseases in regions like Sierra Leone.

The gap

Health messages that speak at communities, not with them

  • Top-down education overlooks local voices and relationships.
  • Biomedical messaging ignores traditional wisdom.
  • Tools such as MomConnect and Pregnancy+ deliver information one way.
  • Direct translation can distort meaning, as with hlonipha, a custom of respectful avoidance speech.
The idea

Oral tradition, carried by digital tools

This study examines how African oral storytelling traditions can be integrated with digital health tools.

Participants will co-design and share interactive narratives that connect individual experiences with collective understanding, and explore how a community's values can inform health strategies.

Research questions

What the study asks

RQ1

Narrative effectiveness

Do locally situated narratives outperform traditional health education in addressing socio-cultural barriers?

RQ2

AI affordances and limits

Can generative AI help non-technical people co-author accurate, culturally aligned stories, including in Krio and Temne?

KrioTemne
Methodology

Sequential co-design

With MothersPrimary-level health workersTraditional birth attendantsEldersStorytellers
  1. 1

    Contextual inquiry and co-design

    Interviews with griots and mothers map existing storytelling traditions, followed by readiness workshops and co-design sessions.

  2. 2

    Prototype development

    A co-designed, culturally responsive interface connects non-technical users to an advanced AI language model.

  3. 3

    Deployment and refinement

    Deploy, test and refine in real-world settings to generate prenatal and postnatal care stories.
